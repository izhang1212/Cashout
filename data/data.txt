Overview: 
- First phase of this project; loads data that we will need to execute our policy

Data:
- Combo: Loads our combo with legs
- Kalshi odds: Loads Kalshi bid stream which we execute our policy against
- Live odds: Loads live odds (we can either use them into our execution policy, or benchmark our own model against it)

Flow:
1. loader.load_seed() reads the YAML into a Seed 
2. artifical_odds.generate_kalshi_series(combo, seed.timeline, seed.reference_data) produces
   the bid stream that the exit policy will trade against, with kalshi_fee supplying the friction
3. odds_api.try_benchmark_odds(...) optionally pulls real odds to grade prediction
